🌿 Survive the Savanna 🌿

Welcome to the Game!

Goal: Survive 4 rounds with the most energy remaining at the highest trophic level.

Rules:

  • Producers start with 100 energy tokens. All other levels start with 10.
  • Each round: Draw an event card, then make a decision (Reproduce, Store, or Hunt/Forage).
  • Energy transfers upward at 10% per round.
  • Round 3 has a Knowledge Checkpoint for bonus energy.
  • Round 4 is a drought - you must predict what happens!
